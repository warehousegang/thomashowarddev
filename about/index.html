About

I'm a platform engineer focused on building and operating production software systems.

My work centers on Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure, internal developer platforms, GitOps, reliability, and AI-assisted software development. I'm particularly interested in the engineering problems that appear after the initial implementation is finished: architecture, failure modes, automation, observability, developer experience, and operating systems in production.

This site is where I write about what I'm building, what I'm learning, and the engineering decisions that survive contact with production.

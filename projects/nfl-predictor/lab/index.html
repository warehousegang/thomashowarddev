What will sit behind a real forecast?

Pregame team-strength features, a validated model, and a forecast saved before kickoff. Each published pick will show its model version and prediction time.

This example uses a two-outcome display. The initial modeling experiment excludes ties; the live version will need an explicit tie policy before predictions are published. The values above are hand-authored to demonstrate the interface.
